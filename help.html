Help

Something not working, or a question this page doesn’t answer? Email help@collecki.com.

Scanning a card

Open the Capture tab and hold a card inside the outline. Collecki recognises it and saves it; there is nothing to tap.

It saved the wrong printing

Tap the card in the row under the camera and pick the right printing. You can delete the card from there too.

Putting cards in a binder

In the Binders tab, tap the plus button to make a binder, and take a photo of the real one if you like. To fill it, choose it under “Scan to” at the top of the Capture tab so new scans go straight in, or open the binder and tap the plus button to add cards you have already scanned.

Removing a card

Press and hold a card. In the Cards tab you can delete it; inside a binder you can take it out of the binder, and it stays in your collection.

Where prices come from

They are market prices from TCGdex, refreshed daily. Collecki can’t tell a card’s finish, so it uses the price of the plainest printing. A price marked ≈ is a European price converted from euros.

Hiding prices

Turn on Sensitive mode in Settings. Every digit of a price is shown as a dot.

Where your cards are saved

On your phone, and backed up to the account you sign in to with Apple. Settings shows whether everything is backed up.
