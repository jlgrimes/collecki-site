Collecki

Scan your cards in a blink.

Collecki recognises a Pokémon card the moment your camera sees it, files it in a binder, and tells you what your collection is worth.

Coming soon to the App Store. For iPhone, iOS 17 or later.

  • Scan

    Point your camera at a card. Collecki knows which one it is and adds it. No typing, no searching.

  • Binders

    Group cards the way a real binder does. Take a photo of yours and it becomes the cover.

  • Worth

    Market prices, refreshed daily, for every card and binder. Hide them when someone is looking.

Sign in with Apple and every card is saved to your account.
